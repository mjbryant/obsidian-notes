## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Wash and slice scallions into 1-inch pieces. Mince garlic. Peel and shred or grate the carrot.
3. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large, preferably non-stick pan over high heat. Add ground beef and cook, breaking up meat, until browned, 3–5 minutes. Season with a big pinch of salt and pepper. Drain excess grease from pan.
4. Add scallion whites and garlic to pan with beef. Cook, stirring, 1–2 minutes.
5. Add cabbage and carrot to pan. Cook, tossing, until vegetables are softened, 3–4 minutes.
6. Stir in soy sauce, sweet soy glaze, stock concentrate, and 0.5 cup water. Bring to a simmer and cook until sauce thickens, 1–2 minutes more.
7. Cook egg noodles in boiling salted water until al dente, about 3 minutes. Drain and add noodles to pan with beef and vegetables. Add butter and sesame oil. Toss until well coated. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
8. Divide beef and noodles between bowls. Garnish with scallion greens. Serve.

## Notes

- Sweet soy glaze can be substituted with hoisin sauce if unavailable.
- Shredded coleslaw mix can replace individual cabbage and carrot for a quicker prep.
- Do not overcook noodles as they will continue to soften when tossed in the sauce.
