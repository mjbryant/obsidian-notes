## Instructions

1. Wash and dry all produce.
2. Trim and discard the root end from the lettuce. Separate the outer leaves and set aside for wraps.
3. Thinly slice 1–2 small center leaves until you have about ⅓ cup shredded lettuce per serving.
4. Drain the pickle and roughly chop. Set aside.
5. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, ketchup, half the mustard, and ½ tsp fry seasoning.
6. Stir in water 1 tsp at a time until the sauce reaches a drizzling consistency. Set aside.
7. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
8. Add the ground beef, breaking up the meat into pieces. Cook 3–5 minutes until browned and almost cooked through.
9. Stir in the stock concentrate, remaining fry seasoning, salt, pepper, and ¼ cup water.
10. Cook, stirring, until the liquid has absorbed and the beef is cooked through, about 2–3 minutes.
11. Add half the shredded cheese and stir until melted, about 1 minute. Pour off any excess grease if needed. Remove from heat.
12. Divide whole lettuce leaves between plates.
13. Fill each wrap with shredded lettuce, beef mixture, chopped pickle, remaining cheese, and crispy fried onions.
14. Drizzle with special sauce and serve.

## Notes

- Ground beef is fully cooked when the internal temperature reaches 160°F.
- Serve family-style in the center of the table and let everyone assemble their own wraps to prevent sogginess, or serve assembled just before eating.
- The sauce recipe makes enough for 2 servings; for 4 servings, use all the mustard and all 1 tsp fry seasoning.
- To scale to 3 servings, make 1.5× the ingredients; for larger batches cook in batches or use a larger pan.
