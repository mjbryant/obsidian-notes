## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan-forced).
2. Place the brown rice and water in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil.
3. Cover with a lid and reduce the heat to medium.
4. Simmer, covered, for 25–30 minutes or until the rice is soft.
5. Drain the rice and transfer to a medium bowl.
6. Meanwhile, place the cherry tomatoes on a lined oven tray.
7. Toss the tomatoes with half the olive oil and all the balsamic vinegar.
8. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Roast in the oven for 25–30 minutes or until tender and roasted.
10. Heat the remaining olive oil in a medium frying pan over medium heat.
11. Add the red onion and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes or until soft.
12. Add the baby spinach and cook, stirring, for 1–2 minutes or until wilted.
13. Transfer the onion and spinach mixture to the bowl with the brown rice.
14. Add the roasted cherry tomatoes, roasted almonds, and parsley to the rice bowl.
15. Toss to combine and season to taste with salt and pepper.
16. Heat the same frying pan over medium-high heat.
17. Add the haloumi slices and cook for 1 minute on each side or until golden.
18. Divide the brown rice salad between bowls and top with haloumi slices.

## Notes

- The 2P quantities are listed; double all ingredients for 4 servings.
- Cook the haloumi just before serving to keep it warm and golden.
- Roasted almonds can be purchased pre-roasted to save time.
