## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C/140°C fan-forced.
2. Coarsely grate half the haloumi using a box grater. Cut the remaining haloumi into 1 cm thick slices and set aside.
3. Squeeze the excess moisture from the grated carrot.
4. Combine the grated carrot with the grated haloumi, plain flour, and egg in a medium bowl.
5. Season with pepper and form the mixture into round fritters, packing tightly with your hands.
6. Heat a little olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
7. Add the fritters and cook for 3–4 minutes on each side, or until golden.
8. Transfer the fritters to an oven tray lined with baking paper and place in the oven to keep warm.
9. Heat a little more olive oil in the same frying pan over medium-high heat.
10. Add the haloumi slices and cook for 1–2 minutes on each side, or until golden.
11. Whisk together equal parts olive oil and lemon juice with the honey in a small bowl.
12. Combine the rocket, cucumber ribbons, and mint leaves in a large bowl.
13. Drizzle the lemon dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
14. Divide and stack the fritters and haloumi slices on plates. Serve with the minted cucumber salad and hummus.

## Notes

- Squeeze as much moisture as possible from the grated carrot before mixing — this helps the fritters hold together and brown better.
- Keep finished fritters warm in the oven while you cook the haloumi slices so everything is ready at the same time.
- The lemon dressing doubles as a dipping sauce for the hummus plate.
